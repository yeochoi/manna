# Calendar

One month of readings on Journal.

- The month in `title` between two round 36 buttons on `chip`.
- Weekday letters in `muted` `label`. Days are 36 circles in `body` inside 40 tall cells.
- A read day fills `soft` with semibold `green-text`. Today has a 1.5 `primary` ring. The selected day fills `primary` with a 3 `halo`. Future days are `chevron` and cannot be tapped.
- Above the calendar two stats, streak and days read this month, in `display` with `small` captions.
