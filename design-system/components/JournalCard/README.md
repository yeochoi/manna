# JournalCard

The selected day under the calendar. Opens that day's reading.

- `surface` card with `radius-card`, 18 by 16 padding.
- The date in `small` `muted` and the range in `title` sit together as one block. The chevron centers on that block, not on the date alone.
- The key message follows in `body`. A day without a summary yet shows a short `muted` line instead.
